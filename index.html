DYLAN McCORMICK

Graphic Design • Photography

Biography

Dylan is a graphic design student from Plainfield who transferred from Joliet Junior College. Dylan developed an interest in art and graphic design early on thanks to his interest in video games and his parents being art teachers. He has been working with the Adobe Creative Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign since high school. He has used his experience with these programs and photography to create logos, product designs, advertisements, and menus. At North Central College, Dylan was responsible for working with the Center for Student Success to design stickers as awards for their new Wall of Achievements. After graduating, he wants to use his graphic design skills to work freelance and for local businesses.

About This Project

This website was created by Dylan McCormick as a student project for ARTD 245: Web Design at North Central College in Naperville, IL, in 2026. All original graphics were created using Photoshop, Illustrator, or Procreate, and the site was hand-coded using HTML and CSS in Dreamweaver. Text content and design theory are adapted from Flux Academy, Tilda Education, and Dribbble articles.
